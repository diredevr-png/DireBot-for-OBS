Gaming hands' own keyboard, mouse, mouse mat, pad and pad stick
(keyboard.glb, mouse.glb, mat.glb, pad.glb, stick.glb), from DireBot for
OBS 0.9.0.

Our own, made from plain shapes by tools/make-gaming-props.py (run
`python3 tools/make-gaming-props.py` to make them again), under the
add-on's licence (GPL-2.0-or-later). No letters, logos or anyone's
product on them.

Each is in metres, as the face avatar's desk has it: x its left as it
faces the character, y up, z away from the character. The pad's middle is
at 0 (its face up), a stick's root at 0 (where it leans from).

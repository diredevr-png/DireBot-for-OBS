Google MediaPipe face, body and hand models (in DireBot for OBS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Licence: Apache-2.0
Copyright The MediaPipe Authors (Google LLC)
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker -
face_landmarker.task (the face finder, Face Mesh V2 and Blendshape V2);
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/holistic_landmarker -
holistic_landmarker.task (the body finder, body model, hand-square model
and hand model) and hand_landmark_lite.tflite (the lighter hand model);

palm_detection_full.tflite (the palm finder, its Hand Landmarker's own);
all unchanged, inside the DireBot for OBS add-on, for the face avatar and
following your hands and arms. DireBot for OBS runs them with its own
code: no MediaPipe or TensorFlow code is in it, or in DireBot.

------------------------------------------------------
DireBot for OBS's own code follows the way these work. None of their code
is in DireBot or DireBot for OBS; they're named here with thanks.

- VMagicMirror (MIT, Copyright (c) baku_dreameater) -
  https://github.com/malaybaku/VMagicMirror - hands on the keyboard,
  mouse and gamepad, the blink, the eyes' small moves and breathing.
- uLipSync (MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 hecomi) -
  https://github.com/hecomi/uLipSync - finding the vowel you're saying
  from the mic, for the mouth.
- EVMC4U, EasyVirtualMotionCaptureForUnity (MIT, Copyright (c) 2019-2020
  gpsnmeajp) - https://github.com/gpsnmeajp/EasyVirtualMotionCaptureForUnity
  - taking in motion over VMC.
- VTubeStudioBlendshapeUDPReceiverTest (MIT, Copyright (c) 2024 Denchi) -
  https://github.com/DenchiSoft/VTubeStudioBlendshapeUDPReceiverTest -
  how VTube Studio's phone app sends your face.
- facetrack-d (BSD-2-Clause, Copyright 2022 Inochi2D Project) -
  https://github.com/Inochi2D/facetrack-d; VTube-IFacial-Link and
  VTube-IFacial-Link-Sharp (MIT, xuan25) -
  https://github.com/xuan25/VTube-IFacial-Link; ExpressionAppBridge
  (MIT, Copyright (c) 2023 DrBomb) -
  https://github.com/DrBomb/ExpressionAppBridge - how a phone's face
